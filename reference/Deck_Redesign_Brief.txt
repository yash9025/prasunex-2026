# LearningBridge Round 2 — Deck Redesign Brief

**Role for the AI building this:** You are a senior presentation designer. You are rebuilding a 12-slide technical design deck for a hackathon jury (Prasunex Innovathon 2026, Round 2). The content is already correct. **Keep every technical fact. Change the design, the layout of every slide, and the amount of text on screen.**

The current deck has one template repeated 12 times: title bar, numbered navy ribbons, three equal boxes, 150+ words per slide. A jury will skim it, not read it. The new deck must be *looked at*, not read.

---

## 0. The 5 rules that override everything

1. **One hero visual per slide.** A diagram, a big number, a mockup, a timeline. If a slide has no hero, redesign it.
2. **No two consecutive slides share a layout.** See the layout map in section 4.
3. **Banned: three equal cards in a row, numbered ribbon headers, stacked bullet boxes, tables with default borders, and "title + subtitle + 3 columns".** Use diagrams, flows, annotated visuals, stat blocks and asymmetric layouts instead.
4. **Max about 40 words of body text per slide**, not counting diagram labels. Move the rest to speaker notes (the existing notes are already good).
5. **Titles are claims, not topics.** "Solution scope and design contract" becomes "A score tells you how much. Not what next." Each slide has a 3-5 word label above the title, so the original topic is still named.

---

## 1. Design concept: "The Bridge at Night"

Dark, confident, a bit editorial. The cover photo is a warm classroom with navy, violet and cyan light, so the whole deck extends that scene.

**Visual motif:** a thin glowing **bridge line** (a curved or straight 2 px line, cyan to violet) that appears on most slides and connects the elements. It stands for *evidence → teacher → support → reassessment*. It is the deck's signature. It runs through diagrams, underlines the title, or links nodes.

**Tone:** precise and calm, with moments of warmth. Technical, but the teacher is always the human at the center.

## 2. Colour system

Dark slides are the default. Use **light slides as relief** on slides 2, 6 and 12 (see the rhythm in section 4).

| Role | Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Background dark | Midnight Ink | `0B1020` | Main dark background |
| Surface dark | Deep Indigo | `151B3A` | Panels, cards, mockup frame |
| Surface line | Slate Edge | `2A3263` | 1 px borders, dividers |
| **Brand** | Electric Violet | `7C5CFF` | Brand, key shapes, "LearningBridge" |
| **System** | Signal Cyan | `22D3EE` | Automation, data flow, the bridge line, code |
| **Teacher / human** | Warm Amber | `FFB547` | **Every teacher or human-approval moment** |
| Success / verified | Mint | `34D399` | Passed checks, approved, safe |
| Risk / failure | Coral | `FF6B6B` | Failure, rejection, risk, 409 errors |
| Text on dark | Soft White | `EEF0FF` | Titles and body on dark |
| Muted text | Mist | `8B93B8` | Labels, captions, footers |
| Light background | Warm Paper | `F6F3EC` | Light-slide background |
| Text on light | Ink | `14172F` | Text on light slides |

### The semantic colour rule (important)

Colour carries meaning, so the deck explains itself:

- **Cyan = the system does it** (import, scoring, queue, API)
- **Amber = a teacher does it** (confirm gap, edit, approve, close)
- **Mint = verified / approved**
- **Coral = failure or risk**
- **Violet = brand and structure only**

Add a tiny legend (Cyan dot "System", Amber dot "Teacher") on slides 3, 4 and 7. Never use amber for anything that is not a human decision.

**Contrast:** all text must be at least 4.5:1. Never put Mist on Deep Indigo for anything under 12 pt. Never use pure `000000` or `FFFFFF`.

## 3. Typography

| Role | Font | Fallback (use if font is not installed) | Size | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Titles | **Sora** | Segoe UI Semibold | 34-40 pt (cover 64 pt) | Semibold / Bold |
| Body | **Inter** | Calibri | 14-16 pt (never below 14) | Regular / Medium |
| Labels / eyebrows | Inter | Calibri | 11-12 pt, ALL CAPS, +8% tracking | Semibold |
| Code, API paths, formulas | **JetBrains Mono** | Consolas | 12-14 pt | Regular |
| Giant numbers | Sora | Segoe UI Semibold | 72-120 pt | Bold |

- Set fonts in the **theme** (heading font, body font), not on every text box.
- Sora, Inter and JetBrains Mono are free Google Fonts. **Install them or embed them**, or the deck will silently fall back on another machine. If you cannot guarantee this, use the fallback column everywhere.
- Hierarchy on every slide: **label (small caps) → title (large) → hero visual → one-line takeaway**. Nothing else competes.
- Left-aligned text only. No centred paragraphs. No underlined titles.
- Line spacing 1.1 for titles, 1.3 for body.

---

## 4. Layout system

- Canvas **16:9, 13.333 × 7.5 in**.
- Margins **0.6 in** left and right, **0.5 in** top, **0.5 in** bottom.
- 12-column grid. Align everything to it. Gaps between elements at least 0.3 in.
- Cards: 12 px corner radius, `151B3A` fill, 1 px `2A3263` border, soft shadow only on the hero element.
- Backgrounds: flat `0B1020` plus **one soft radial glow** (violet or cyan, 15-25% opacity) placed in a different corner each slide, supplied as a pre-rendered image because gradients are not natively supported by the library.
- Footer (identical on every slide, 10 pt Mist): `LearningBridge · Proposed design · Synthetic data` on the left, slide number on the right. This **replaces** the repeated disclaimers.
- **No** "ROUND 2" tag top-right. **No** repeated brand text in the header.
- Icons: one consistent line-icon set (Lucide or Feather), 2 px stroke, cyan or amber only. No emoji. No clip art.

## 6. Build and production rules

- Build with native shapes and native text. **Keep charts and tables native** where a real one is needed. Diagrams are made of shapes and connectors so the team can edit them.
- Use **theme fonts and theme colours**, set once, so the whole deck can be restyled in one change.
- Use a **slide master / layouts** for the footer and slide number. Do not paste them on each slide.
- Hex colours with no `#`. Remember that transparency is a separate property from the colour.
- Add **alt text** to the photo, mockup and every diagram.
- **Animations:** optional and subtle only. Fade in diagram stages left to right on slides 3, 7 and 11. Nothing spinning, bouncing or flying.
- **Speaker notes:** keep the existing notes (they hold the honest caveats and sources). Delete these lines from the notes because they should not be seen:
  - "Visual structure follows the user-supplied OfferLens technical brief reference..." (all slides)
  - The local file path `C:/Users/YASH/Desktop/...` (slide 1)
  - "Team shortlisting is user-reported." (check that you want this one visible)

## 7. Quality checklist (run before delivering)

- [ ] Every slide has **one obvious hero visual** and a clear reading order.
- [ ] **No two neighbouring slides look alike.** Check the thumbnail grid.
- [ ] **No three-equal-card rows** and no numbered ribbon headers anywhere.
- [ ] Body text is **at least 14 pt**, labels at least 11 pt, and nothing is cut off or overflowing.
- [ ] Amber appears **only** for teacher / human moments. Coral only for risk or failure.
- [ ] Margins at least 0.5 in. Gaps at least 0.3 in. Everything sits on the grid.
- [ ] Footer and slide number identical on all slides.
- [ ] Contrast is readable at the back of a room (squint test).
- [ ] Every technical fact from the original deck is still present on a slide or in the notes.
- [ ] Render every slide to an image and **look at each one** before handing it over.
